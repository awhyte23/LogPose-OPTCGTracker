Google Sheets sync

Every card you add and every price you refresh gets pushed to a Sheet automatically — one row per card, updated in place, with a running highest/lowest price. This needs a small Apps Script Web App on your end (I gave you the file for this — paste it in, deploy, and drop the URL here).

One-time cleanup: finds cards added before language tracking existed that ended up duplicated alongside a newer English entry, merges their quantities together, and removes the old duplicate. Won't touch anything that's actually Japanese, or legacy cards with no EN counterpart to merge into.
Reads every card currently in the sheet, wipes the rows, then re-adds each one fresh with a clean key and current price. Fixes accumulated corruption without re-scanning anything. Resets Highest/Lowest Price Seen to today's price for every card \u2014 you lose that history.
Unlike "Refresh prices" on the Collection tab, this works directly on the Sheet itself \u2014 it covers every card ever added, from any device, not just what's loaded on this one. It can take a minute or two for large collections; keep this tab open until it finishes.
